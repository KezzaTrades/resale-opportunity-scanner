In development · UK

Resale Opportunity Scanner

A product comparison and resale analytics project designed to monitor pricing and availability from legitimate retailers, match products to resale-market listings, and highlight potential price differences for manual review.

Trusted retail sourcing

Focuses on established and authorised retailers rather than unknown marketplace sellers.

Price comparison

Compares current retail pricing with resale-market information at product and variant level where available.

Opportunity analytics

Estimates fees, margins and ROI so products can be prioritised for manual purchasing decisions.

What the platform is being built to do

  • Monitor products, sale prices and availability from legitimate UK retailers.
  • Match products using identifiers such as SKU, style code or GTIN where available.
  • Compare retailer pricing with resale-market bids, asks and other market data.
  • Estimate transaction fees, acquisition cost, potential margin and return on investment.
  • Surface high-priority opportunities in a simple comparison dashboard.
  • Direct users to the original retailer product page for manual review and purchase.

Data approach

The project is intended to use official APIs, authorised affiliate/product feeds and other permitted structured retailer data wherever available. Integrations are added only where access and usage terms permit.

Current status: prototype / integration stage. Retailer and resale-market data connections are being evaluated and requested.
Disclosure: This site is an independent analytics project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by StockX or any retailer mentioned through future integrations. Product availability, prices and resale values can change. Any purchasing decision remains manual.
